# Company IT Security Policy

## Password Policy
All employees must use complex passwords with a minimum length of 12 characters. Passwords must include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
Passwords should be updated every 90 days.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
MFA is required for all remote access, admin accounts, and VPN connections. Supported MFA methods include authenticator apps and hardware tokens.

## Data Backup and Recovery
All critical data must be backed up daily to the secure cloud storage system. Backup logs are reviewed weekly. Recovery drills are performed quarterly.

## Acceptable Use Policy
Company devices and networks must be used for authorized business purposes only. Personal use is permitted during breaks if it does not interfere with business activities.

## Email and Communication
Phishing awareness training is mandatory for all employees annually. Do not open suspicious attachments or click unknown links. Use secure messaging platforms for sensitive data.

## Software and Patch Management
Only approved software may be installed on company devices. Automatic patching is enabled and managed centrally by the IT department.

## Incident Response
In case of a security breach, report immediately to it-security@example.com. The incident response team will investigate and take corrective action within 24 hours.
