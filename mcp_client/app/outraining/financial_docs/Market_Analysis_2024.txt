2024 Market Analysis
====================

The global equity markets saw volatility in Q1 2024 amid rising interest rates.
Banking sector maintained strong credit quality; non-performing loans remained < 1%.
Tech spending rebounded in Europe, with 5G rollouts accelerating demand.

Analysts forecast moderate GDP growth of 2.8% in North America for 2024.2024 Market Analysis
