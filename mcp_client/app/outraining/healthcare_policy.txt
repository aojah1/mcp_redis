Healthcare policies are structured frameworks that define access to medical services, treatment affordability, and system efficiency. Policies may cover preventive care, chronic disease management, electronic health records, telemedicine, and public health surveillance. Government reforms like universal health coverage aim to balance cost, quality, and accessibility. The role of digital health and AI in diagnostics and decision support is rapidly growing, demanding data privacy regulations and interoperability standards.
