Finance Policy – Company X

1. Budget Allocation:
   - Each department must submit quarterly budget proposals.
   - Emergency funds require CFO approval.

2. Expense Guidelines:
   - Travel expenses must be pre-approved and reimbursed via the finance portal.
   - Purchases over $10,000 require two levels of authorization.

3. Reporting:
   - Monthly financial reports are due by the 5th of each month.
   - Audits are conducted twice a year and require full departmental cooperation.
