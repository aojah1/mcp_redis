Customer Support Guidelines – Company X

1. Response Times:
   - Emails: Must be responded to within 24 hours.
   - Chats and voice calls: Must be responded to within **2 minutes**.

2. Issue Resolution:
   - Tier 1 resolves common FAQs and product inquiries.
   - Tier 2 handles escalated issues, including technical troubleshooting.

3. Documentation:
   - Every customer interaction must be logged in the CRM.
   - Escalations must include detailed notes and resolution attempts.

4. Language & Courtesy:
   - Use empathetic, professional language.
   - Avoid jargon unless necessary, and always confirm customer understanding.
